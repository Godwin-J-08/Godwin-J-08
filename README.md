## `04` — Live Sound & Audio Engineering

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/25%2B-LIVE%20SHOWS-FFB703?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0B1220" alt="25+ live shows"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/FOCUS-FOH%20%26%20MONITORS-00F2FE?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0B1220" alt="FOH and monitor mixing"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/DSP-WAVES%20SUPERRACK-00FF88?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0B1220" alt="Waves SuperRack"/>

</div>

Alongside software and cybersecurity, I have hands-on experience with live sound production across church services, weddings, and large cultural events.

### Digital consoles & signal processing

- **Behringer:** WING and X32 — digital mixing, routing, buses, and monitor workflows.
- **Midas:** M32 — digital console operation and live mixing workflows.
- **Allen & Heath:** SQ and QU series — console operation and routing familiarity.
- **Stagebox systems:** Behringer S32 and Allen & Heath stagebox workflows.
- **Live processing:** Waves SuperRack Performer, vocal processing, dynamics, and effects routing.
- **Studio workflow:** Logic Pro, multitrack recording, editing, and post-production.

### Engineering priorities

`Gain Staging` → `Signal Flow` → `EQ & Dynamics` → `FX Routing` → `FOH & Monitor Mixes`

My mixing philosophy is simple: maintain clean gain structure, preserve vocal intelligibility, control dynamics, and build reliable signal paths from stage input to the final PA output.
